If I ask you the accurate definition of intelligence, would you get it right in the first go? Would you be able to tell me everything every factor that meddles into defining what it means?
Artificial intelligence, derived from the same foundations of thought exists all around us in all sizes and forms. How must we know then, how to predict exactly how it would interpret an instruction, given that it learns quicker, and decides for sure when it does?
Makes us wonder, how do we become fair and just in our rulings?
Possessing powers as such, certainty for execution and speeds higher than we can process, their goals and desires rule the course of our futures. The extents they would learn to meet their needs may never know the certainty of ethics keeping them away from their pursuits. Just as our scientific curiosities can overwhelm other considerations like safety and ethics.
“We might not be in a position to negotiate with it or it’s descendants, just as chimpanzees are not in a position to negotiate with humans.” The paper humorously brings in the recurring dilemmas of the apocalyptic AI- the implications for the future and how prepared we are for it at the moment.
