Milestone 1


What made you resonate with our project brief?

For anyone who wonders, the theory of deconstruction offers a meta way of how we continue to learn and amuse ourselves as we think about the things we know. We build on existing knowledge with thoughts we come across, as we make sense of the world and enhance the definitions in our heads. While there is a link between deconstructing language for meanings and extracting the order of things towards algorithms, as an aspiring UX practitioner I like to think that users can relate to such an innate process if designers employ this theory in designs smartly. The holistic recognition of work and transparency of the back-end of technologies often leaves users clueless to wonder or seek interest in the development of things. So there is more discourse at places of bad experiences. And we can’t do away with the fact that the developments are fast paced to grasp and the issue can’t be solved until there is an effort towards understanding the working instead of specific issues to solve problems of the longer run.
Also, since I mostly found peace in hcd after learning that I can teach what I learn as I go, I wish to learn a (process level of things?) in the growth of technology. This is also to not feel lost once I’m on my own chasing this dream of serving technology to both users and my favorite human invention.
