This week was a balance of conceptualisation and making. The switch between projects in order to make both progress and execute the making of both felt abrupt at first but later made sense- planning to feeling first abrupt later made sense. And the idea of time being not enough for the process was gone once making helped build better understanding and execution of the itself. The divergence of thoughts to converging them into concepts can also be seen as the ‘discomfort’ in the creative process. Bringing the ideas fit to real factors involves empathy and careful consideration of significance, to say how much of a value unfolds with an idea. I mistook this process for confusion earlier, only to know later that it is part of the process. Real life application carries a sense of responsibility that is powerful to facilitate the entire process. The problem is being confident within all the uncertainty, or is it the solution?

Amused by the thought of being bored making us creative while cutting a lot of paper, I made sure I did monotonous tasks in my breaks to test the hypothesis- it works!
