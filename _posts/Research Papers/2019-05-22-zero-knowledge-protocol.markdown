Here’s a dilemma: If you possess some extremely valuable information, and wish the world would acknowledge it without you having to reveal any information about it to them, how can you do it?
Ofcourse, you would need to give the audience some assertion for them to believe you. And you would have to ensure that you don’t reveal anything more than ‘I know what I know!’.
